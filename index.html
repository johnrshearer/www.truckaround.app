Connecting hungry people with great food

Current status

The TruckAround service is still in early development. We expect to have a working prototype before October 31, 2026.


What is TruckAround?

Hungry people can't find a food truck nearby, and they can't follow their favorite truck. We want to fix that.

How does it work?

With food trucks' collaboration, we will track where and when they are serving food in real time and display them directly on the TruckAround mobile map.

Where do I join?

For Food Trucks

You will be able to download the TruckAround mobile app for iOS or Android to broadcast your location live.

For Consumers

You'll be able to find nearby trucks on the iOS/Android app or directly on the web.
